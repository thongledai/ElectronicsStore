Our story

A gadget store built by people who actually use the gadgets

TechNova started in 2019 as a two-person repair counter in Patiala. Customers kept asking the same question — "where can I buy this without worrying it's a fake?" — and we kept not having a good answer. So we became the answer.

Today we stock 25 carefully chosen products across five categories. That number is deliberately small. Every item on this site has been unboxed, used and argued about by our team before it earned a place in the catalogue. If we would not recommend it to a friend, we do not sell it.

What we stand for

Four promises we don't break

These aren't posters on a wall — they're the rules we use to decide what to stock and how to handle problems.

Authenticity first

Every unit is sourced from the brand or an authorised national distributor. Serial numbers are verifiable, and warranty claims go through official channels.

Honest pricing

The price you see includes tax. No handling fees appear at checkout, and we never inflate an MRP just to advertise a bigger discount.

Support that helps

Our support team are technicians, not script readers. They can actually diagnose your problem, and they have authority to issue a replacement on the spot.

Responsible e-waste

We take back any old device you buy a replacement for, free of charge, and route it to a certified recycler. Packaging is plastic-free by default.

Our journey

From a repair counter to 50,000 orders

Growth has been deliberately slow. We have turned down distribution deals that would have forced us to stock products we do not believe in, and we would do it again.

2019
The repair counter

Two engineers, one table, and a growing queue of people who couldn't find genuine parts anywhere in the city.

2021
First online storefront

We listed 12 products and sold out of all of them in six weeks. The catalogue has stayed intentionally small ever since.

2023
Same-city 24-hour delivery

Our own delivery fleet launched in Patiala and Chandigarh, cutting average delivery time from four days to under a day.

2025
Nationwide shipping

Partnering with three logistics providers let us reach every serviceable PIN code in India within four business days.

2026
50,000 orders and counting

A 98% satisfaction rate and a return rate under 2% — numbers we're prouder of than the order count itself.
